1. ACCOUNT PURPOSE & TARGET AUDIENCE
Meridian Personal Savings Accounts are designed for resident individuals, households, and professionals seeking secure liquid fund storage with electronic payment access.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR OPENING AN ACCOUNT
To open a personal savings account at Meridian National Bank, applicants must meet the following eligibility conditions:
- Age Qualification: Any resident individual who is 18 years of age or older and competent to contract is eligible to open a standard single or joint personal savings account.
- Minor Accounts: Minors aged 10 years and older may operate an independent minor savings account with daily withdrawal caps of ₹2,500; minors under 10 years must be operated jointly with natural or legal court-appointed guardians.
- Residential Status: Resident Indian individuals. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) must apply under the dedicated NRE/NRO policy guidelines.

3. MANDATORY KYC DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST
Applicants must furnish one document from each category under RBI Customer Due Diligence regulations:
- Identity & Address Proof (Officially Valid Document - OVD): Valid Passport, Aadhaar Card with masked UID, Voter Identity Card, or Driving Licence.
- Tax Identification: Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card or Form 60 declaration where PAN has not yet been assigned.
- Recent Photograph: One passport-size color photograph (or live digital webcam capture during Video-KYC).
- Video-KYC (V-KYC) Option: Fully digital instant account opening available through the Meridian Mobile App 24/7 with zero physical branch visits required.

4. AVERAGE MONTHLY BALANCE (AMB) REQUIREMENTS
Meridian Bank calculates Average Monthly Balance based on the cumulative end-of-day ledger balance divided by the number of calendar days in the month:
- Metro & Urban Branches: Mandatory Average Monthly Balance of ₹2,500.
- Semi-Urban & Rural Branches: Mandatory Average Monthly Balance of ₹1,000.
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA / Zero Balance): Nil minimum balance requirement with basic transaction caps.

5. NON-MAINTENANCE CHARGES (NMC)
If the Average Monthly Balance is not maintained, quarterly notification is dispatched prior to debiting non-maintenance charges:
- Metro/Urban Shortfall: ₹150 + 18% GST per month for shortfalls up to 50%; ₹250 + GST per month for shortfalls exceeding 50%.
- Semi-Urban/Rural Shortfall: ₹75 + GST per month for shortfalls up to 50%; ₹125 + GST per month for shortfalls exceeding 50%.

6. ACCOUNT PRIVILEGES & INCLUDED COMPLIMENTARY BENEFITS
- Meridian Contactless Debit Card: Issued with EMV chip protection and complimentary domestic airport lounge access (1 visit per quarter).
- Cheque Book Facilities: One complimentary 25-leaf personalised cheque book per financial year. Additional leaves charged at ₹3 per cheque leaf.
- Digital Banking: Free lifetime access to Meridian Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, UPI payments, and NEFT/RTGS online transfers.
- Interest Payment: Savings account interest of 3.50% p.a. calculated daily and credited quarterly directly into the account.
