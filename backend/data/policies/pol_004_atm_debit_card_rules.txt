1. POLICY SCOPE & CARD ISSUANCE
This policy defines the transaction thresholds, daily usage caps, inter-bank interchange guidelines, and penalty fees for Meridian National Bank debit and ATM cards.

2. DAILY TRANSACTION LIMIT SCHEDULE
Standard Meridian Classic and Platinum Debit Cards operate under the following prescribed daily velocity limits:
- Daily ATM Cash Withdrawal Limit: ₹50,000 per day at all Meridian ATMs and affiliated National Financial Switch (NFS) automated teller machines.
- Per-Transaction ATM Limit: ₹10,000 per single ATM withdrawal dispensing cycle (up to 5 transactions permitted daily to reach ₹50,000 cap).
- Daily POS (Point of Sale) Merchant Limit: ₹1,00,000 per day at physical retail merchant terminals.
- Daily E-Commerce / Online Transaction Limit: ₹1,00,000 per day with mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (OTP / Token).
- Contactless Tap-and-Pay Limit: ₹5,000 per transaction without entering PIN; transactions exceeding ₹5,000 require EMV chip insertion and PIN authentication.
- Micro-ATM & Cash@POS Withdrawal Limit: ₹2,000 per transaction with an aggregate daily cap of ₹5,000 across partner banking correspondents.

3. INTER-BANK ATM CHARGES & FREE TRANSACTION ALLOWANCE
- Meridian Own ATMs: 100% unlimited free financial and non-financial transactions for all Meridian account holders.
- Other Bank ATMs (Metro Locations - Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad): 3 free transactions per calendar month.
- Other Bank ATMs (Non-Metro Locations): 5 free transactions per calendar month.
- Beyond Free Limits: ₹21 + 18% GST per financial withdrawal; ₹10 + GST per non-financial inquiry (balance inquiry / mini-statement).

4. DEBIT CARD SCHEDULE OF CHARGES & REPLACEMENT FEES
- New Card Issuance Fee: Nil (Complimentary welcome card issued at account opening).
- Card Replacement Fee (Lost, Stolen, Damaged, or Demagnetised Card): Fixed fee of ₹200 + 18% GST (Total ₹236) debited upon replacement dispatch.
- Physical PIN Regeneration Fee: ₹50 + GST at branch counter; Nil fee for instant Green PIN generation via Meridian Mobile App or ATM.
- Annual Debit Card Maintenance Fee (AMC): Nil for the first 12 months; ₹150 + 18% GST billed annually at the start of each subsequent year.
- International Transaction Mark-up: 3.50% cross-currency conversion mark-up on foreign currency merchant and ATM transactions.

5. SECURITY PROTOCOLS & INSTANT HOTLISTING (BLOCKING)
In case of debit card loss, theft, skimming suspicion, or unauthorised transaction alerts, customers must immediately block the card through one of the following official channels:
- 24/7 SMS Hotlisting: Send SMS `BLOCK <Last 4 digits of card>` to 56161 from the registered mobile number. Instant confirmation SMS is received within 15 seconds.
- Meridian Mobile App: Navigate to Cards > Card Controls > Toggle "Lock Card" or select "Permanently Block & Reissue".
- Toll-Free Emergency Helpline: Dial 1800-425-6622 for immediate human agent blocking assistance.
- Zero Liability Protection: Customer liability is zero for unauthorised electronic transactions reported within 3 working days of occurrence.
