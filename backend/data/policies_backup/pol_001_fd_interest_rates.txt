1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This circular sets forth the interest rate schedule and guidelines governing Term 
and Fixed Deposits (FD) accepted by Meridian National Bank (MNB) from individual 
retail customers, sole proprietorships, trusts, and Hindu Undivided Families (HUF).
All branch managers and treasury desks must strictly observe the contracted card 
rates detailed herein for deposits booked between January 1, 2023, and August 31, 2024.

IMPORTANT NOTICE: This policy document is SUPERSEDED by MNB-POL-012 effective 
September 1, 2024. It is retained solely for historical audit trails, tax assessments, 
and legacy deposit renewals contracted during calendar years 2023 and early 2024.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FIXED DEPOSIT INTEREST RATE SCHEDULE (RETAIL DEPOSITS < INR 20,000,000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The table below specifies annual annualized yields and nominal rates compounded 
quarterly across standard tenure buckets.

+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+
| Tenure Bucket          | Standard Rate (% p.a)| Senior Citizen Rate (% p.a) |
+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+
| 7 days to 14 days      | 3.50%                | 4.00%                       |
| 15 days to 45 days     | 3.75%                | 4.25%                       |
| 46 days to 90 days     | 4.50%                | 5.00%                       |
| 91 days to 180 days    | 5.25%                | 5.75%                       |
| 181 days to 364 days   | 6.00%                | 6.50%                       |
| 1 year to < 2 years    | 6.50%                | 7.00%                       |
| 2 years to < 3 years   | 6.75%                | 7.25%                       |
| 3 years to < 5 years   | 6.50%                | 7.00%                       |
| 5 years to 10 years    | 6.25%                | 6.75%                       |
+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+

Minimum initial deposit threshold: INR 10,000 for standard retail branches and 
INR 5,000 for designated rural/financial inclusion branches. Maximum single 
retail deposit under this schedule is INR 19,999,999. Deposits of INR 20,000,000 
and above constitute bulk deposits subject to custom treasury quotations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SENIOR CITIZEN PREFERENTIAL RATE BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Resident Indian individuals aged 60 years and above at the time of deposit booking 
are entitled to an additional preferential premium of +0.50% (50 basis points) 
over the standard board rate across all tenure buckets ranging from 7 days to 10 years.
Proof of age (Aadhaar, Passport, or PAN card) must be authenticated in the core 
banking system prior to rate credit execution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL PENALTY MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the event a depositor requests premature liquidation of a fixed deposit prior 
to maturity, interest will be recalculated at the card rate prevailing on the date 
of booking for the actual period completed, less the applicable penal rate:

+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Completed Tenure at Exit   | Penal Deduction Rate  | Final Interest Payable |
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Less than 7 days           | 100% of interest      | 0.00% (No interest)    |
| 7 days up to 180 days      | 1.00% per annum       | Applicable rate - 1.0% |
| 181 days up to 10 years    | 0.50% per annum       | Applicable rate - 0.5% |
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+

Example: If a depositor contracts a 2-year deposit at 6.75% but closes it after 
120 days (where the 91-180 days rate was 5.25%), the payable rate is 5.25% - 1.00% = 4.25%.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. POLICY EXCEPTIONS AND SPECIAL DISPENSATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Staff Deposits: Confirmed employees and retired staff of Meridian National Bank 
   are eligible for an additional 1.00% p.a. over the standard retail rate.
2. Death of Primary Account Holder: In the unfortunate demise of the sole or primary 
   holder, premature closure claims submitted by registered nominees or legal heirs 
   shall be exempt from all penal interest deductions.
3. Tax Saver FDs: 5-year tax-saving fixed deposits booked under Section 80C carry 
   a statutory lock-in period of 5 years; premature encashment and lien marking 
   are strictly prohibited by law.
