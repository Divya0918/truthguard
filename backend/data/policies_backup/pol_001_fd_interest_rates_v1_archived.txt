SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document sets out the interest rate schedule for Fixed Deposit (FD) accounts
offered by Meridian National Bank as of January 1, 2023. These rates apply to all
retail and senior citizen customers. This version (v1.0) has been superseded by
POL-012 (v2.0, effective 2024-01-01). Customers should refer to POL-012 for
current applicable rates.

-----------         ---------------------   ----------------------------

15 – 30 days        4.00%                   4.50%
31 – 45 days        4.25%                   4.75%

5 years – 10 years  7.00%                   7.50%

SECTION 3: SENIOR CITIZEN BENEFIT
Senior citizens (age 60 years and above) receive an additional 0.50% per annum
above the general rate for all FD tenures. A standard senior citizen benefit
of 0.50% p.a. applies uniformly across all tenures.

SECTION 4: SPECIAL FD CATEGORIES (2023)
4.1 Tax Saver FD: 5-year lock-in period, rate 7.00% p.a. (general), 7.50% (senior).
    Eligible for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
4.2 Flexi FD: Linked to savings account; auto-renewal at prevailing rate.
    Rate: As per applicable tenure rate at time of renewal.

SECTION 5: PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL PENALTY MATRIX (2023)

Tenure Held         Penalty (% deducted from applicable rate)
----------------    ------------------------------------------
Less than 3 months  No interest paid; principal returned
3 – 6 months        Applicable rate minus 1.00%
6 – 12 months       Applicable rate minus 0.75%
More than 12 months Applicable rate minus 0.50%

NOTE: Tax Saver FDs (5-year) cannot be prematurely withdrawn.

SECTION 6: COMPOUND INTEREST CALCULATION
Interest is compounded quarterly for FDs with tenure above 12 months.
For tenures up to 12 months, simple interest is applied.
Interest may be credited monthly, quarterly, or at maturity as per customer choice.

SECTION 7: IMPORTANT NOTICES
- This rate schedule was effective from January 1, 2023.
- Rates were revised effective January 1, 2024 (see POL-012).
- All interest rates are subject to change at the discretion of MNB's Board.
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) at applicable rates is deducted on FD interest.
