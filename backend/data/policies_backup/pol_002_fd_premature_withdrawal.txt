SECTION 1: GENERAL WITHDRAWAL RULES

1.1 Minimum tenure before withdrawal:
    Standard FD: No minimum (any time)
    Tax Saver FD (POL-012 MNB Shield-5): Cannot be withdrawn before 5-year lock-in
    
1.2 Premature withdrawal request: Must be submitted in writing at branch or via NetBanking
    for FDs up to INR 5,00,000. Above INR 5,00,000 requires branch visit.

SECTION 2: INTEREST PAYABLE ON PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL

The interest paid on premature withdrawal is calculated at the LOWER of:
  (a) The contracted rate at time of FD booking, OR
  (b) The rate applicable for the tenure the FD has actually been held,
  MINUS the applicable penalty.

SECTION 3: PENALTY MATRIX

Tenure Actually Held    Penalty Deduction
--------------------    -----------------------------------------------------------
Less than 3 months      No interest paid. Original principal returned in full.
3 months to 6 months    1.00% deducted from the applicable rate (clause 2 above)
6 months to 12 months   0.75% deducted from the applicable rate
12 months to 24 months  0.50% deducted from the applicable rate
24 months and above     0.50% deducted from the applicable rate

Example 1: FD booked at 7.00% for 3 years. Withdrawn after 7 months.
  Rate for 7 months = 5.75% (from POL-012 schedule: 181–270 days band)
  Penalty = 0.75%
  Interest paid = 5.75% - 0.75% = 5.00% p.a. for 7 months

Example 2: FD booked at 6.75% for 2 years. Withdrawn after 4 months.
  Rate for 4 months = 5.25% (91–120 days band), contracted = 6.75%
  Lower = 5.25%. Penalty = 1.00%. Interest paid = 4.25% p.a. for 4 months.

SECTION 4: PARTIAL WITHDRAWAL
FDs of INR 25,000 and above may be partially withdrawn.
Minimum partial withdrawal: INR 10,000 or 25% of FD principal (whichever is higher).
Remaining principal continues as FD at original contracted rate and tenure.
Partial withdrawal not available for Tax Saver FDs.

SECTION 5: LOAN AGAINST FD (ALTERNATIVE TO PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL)
Customers may avail overdraft / loan against FD up to 90% of FD value.
Rate on loan against FD: 1.00% above the contracted FD rate.
This avoids premature withdrawal penalty and FD continues to earn full interest.

SECTION 6: AUTO-RENEWAL
FDs not claimed within 14 days of maturity are automatically renewed for the same
tenure at the rate prevailing on the maturity date.
