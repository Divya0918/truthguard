1. PURPOSE AND POLICY SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes comprehensive operational rules governing minimum and 
maximum allowable tenures, premature liquidation parameters, partial withdrawal 
options, lock-in rules, and auto-renewal mechanisms for all domestic Fixed 
Deposits (FDs) contracted at Meridian National Bank (MNB).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TENURE PARAMETERS AND LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meridian National Bank accepts fixed term deposits under the following strict 
tenure boundaries:

+-----------------------------+----------------------+------------------------+
| Deposit Classification      | Minimum Tenure       | Maximum Tenure         |
+-----------------------------+----------------------+------------------------+
| Standard Term Deposit       | 7 Days               | 10 Years (120 Months)  |
| Tax-Saving Deposit (80C)    | 5 Years (60 Months)  | 5 Years (60 Months)    |
| Flexi-Sweep Fixed Deposit   | 15 Days              | 1 Year (12 Months)     |
| Recurring Deposit (RD)      | 6 Months             | 10 Years (120 Months)  |
+-----------------------------+----------------------+------------------------+

Deposits under 7 days in duration are legally impermissible for term deposit 
classification under reserve guidelines and shall be declined by core banking.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL & PENALTY MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depositors may request liquidation of Fixed Deposits prior to contracted maturity 
dates subject to the following regulatory framework:

1. Sub-7 Day Liquidations: No interest whatever is payable on deposits liquidated 
   within 6 days from date of booking. Principal is refunded in full without fee.
2. Premature Liquidation Rate Formula: The interest rate applicable shall be the 
   lesser of:
   (a) The contracted rate of the original deposit, or
   (b) The rate prevailing on the booking date for the tenure during which the 
       deposit actually remained with the bank, minus applicable penal haircut.

+--------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------+
| Deposit Amount Bracket   | Completed Duration | Premature Penalty Deduction |
+--------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------+
| Less than INR 500,000    | 7 to 180 days      | 0.50% reduction in yield    |
| Less than INR 500,000    | > 180 days         | 0.50% reduction in yield    |
| INR 500,000 to 20,000,000| 7 to 365 days      | 1.00% reduction in yield    |
| INR 500,000 to 20,000,000| > 1 year           | 0.75% reduction in yield    |
+--------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PARTIAL WITHDRAWAL RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MNB offers partial withdrawal facilities on standard fixed deposits exceeding 
INR 50,000 in total value:
- Increments: Partial encashment must be made in multiples of INR 5,000.
- Residual Balance: The remaining principal balance after withdrawal must not fall 
  below the mandatory minimum threshold of INR 10,000.
- Interest Mechanics: The withdrawn portion incurs premature penalty rules as 
  stipulated in Section 3, while the retained principal continues to earn original 
  contracted interest until original maturity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STATUTORY LOCK-IN PERIODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Tax Saver Scheme (Section 80C): Lock-in duration is exactly 60 calendar months. 
   No premature redemption, loan lien, or overdraft facility is allowed.
2. Court Attachment & Legal Liens: Where a deposit is frozen under statutory order 
   or loan collateral lien, withdrawal is strictly blocked until formal release.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. AUTO-RENEWAL AND MATURITY INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depositors select maturity mandates at account opening:
- Auto-Renewal (Principal + Interest): Full maturity proceeds roll into a new deposit 
  of equal tenure at prevailing board rates on renewal date.
- Auto-Renewal (Principal Only): Interest is credited to linked savings/current account.
- Payout at Maturity: Funds are auto-transferred to linked CASA via NEFT/RTGS.
Grace Period: A 14-calendar-day grace period is provided post-maturity during which 
customers can close or restructure renewed deposits without premature penalty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Senior Citizens: Penalty for premature withdrawal is waived by 0.25% (e.g. 1.0% becomes 0.75%).
- Demise of Account Holder: Complete penalty waiver for nominee liquidation.
