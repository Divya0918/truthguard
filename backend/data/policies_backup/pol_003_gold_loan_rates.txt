SECTION 1: PURPOSE
This policy governs the interest rates, processing fees, and repayment options
for gold loans disbursed by Meridian National Bank to eligible customers.

SECTION 2: INTEREST RATE SCHEDULE — GOLD LOANS

2.1 Standard Gold Loan Rates (Bullet / Overdraft Scheme)

LTV Ratio               Tenure        Rate (% p.a.)
---------------------   ----------    -------------
Up to 65% LTV           Up to 6 mo    9.50%
Up to 65% LTV           6–12 months   10.00%
65.01% to 70% LTV       Up to 6 mo    10.50%
65.01% to 70% LTV       6–12 months   11.00%
70.01% to 75% LTV (max) Up to 6 mo    11.50%
70.01% to 75% LTV (max) 6–12 months   12.00%

Note: Maximum LTV ratio permitted is 75% as per RBI guidelines.
Gold loans exceeding 75% LTV are not sanctioned under any scheme.

2.2 EMI Gold Loan Rates (Reducing Balance)

Tenure              Rate (% p.a.)   Processing Fee
-----------         -------------   --------------
12 months           10.50%          1.00% of loan
18 months           11.00%          1.00% of loan
24 months           11.50%          1.25% of loan
36 months (max)     12.00%          1.50% of loan

Processing fee is charged upfront and is non-refundable.

SECTION 3: REPAYMENT SCHEMES

3.1 Bullet Repayment: Interest paid monthly; principal repaid at end of tenure.
    Available for tenures up to 12 months.
    Interest reset: Quarterly at prevailing rate if principal is not repaid.

3.2 EMI Scheme: Equal monthly installments covering principal + interest.
    Prepayment allowed with 2% prepayment charge on outstanding principal.
    No prepayment charge after 12 months of EMI payments.

3.3 Overdraft (OD) Scheme: Credit limit up to 75% LTV; interest charged on
    actual utilization only. Rate: 12.50% p.a.
    Available for loan amounts INR 1,00,000 and above.

SECTION 4: PROCESSING FEES AND CHARGES

Fee Type                    Charge
-----------------------     --------------------------------
Processing fee (Bullet)     0.50% of loan amount (min INR 500)
Processing fee (EMI)        1.00% – 1.50% (tenure-based)
Valuation fee               INR 250 per packet of gold
Documentation charges       INR 200 (one-time)
Renewal fee                 0.25% of loan amount
Penal interest              2.00% p.a. over contracted rate
Pre-payment (within 12 mo.) 2.00% on outstanding principal
Pre-payment (after 12 mo.)  Nil

SECTION 5: LOAN AMOUNTS
Minimum gold loan: INR 10,000
Maximum gold loan: INR 50,00,000 (subject to LTV and gold purity assessment)
Loan disbursed in: Account credit or demand draft (no cash above INR 20,000)
