SECTION 1: BORROWER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

1.1 Age: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 75 years at loan maturity.
1.2 Citizenship: Indian nationals and NRIs with valid OCI/PIO documentation.
1.3 Occupation: All categories — salaried, self-employed, agriculturists, students
    (with co-applicant), homemakers (with guarantor).
1.4 Existing MNB relationship: Not mandatory; new-to-bank customers accepted.

SECTION 2: GOLD ELIGIBILITY STANDARDS

2.1 Acceptable Gold Articles:
    - Gold jewellery (including studs and ornaments)
    - Gold coins (up to 50 grams per customer as per RBI guidelines)
    - Gold bars (from approved refineries only; LBMA-accredited)

2.2 Purity Standards:
    Minimum purity accepted: 18 karat (75% pure gold)
    Maximum purity standard: 24 karat (99.9% pure gold)
    Purity assessment: Conducted by MNB-certified valuers at the branch.
    Stone deduction: Weight of embedded stones (diamonds, rubies, etc.) is deducted
    before valuation. Stone weights are assessed at 0.5 grams per visible stone.

2.3 Gold Valuation Method:
    Gold is valued at the average of the closing prices published by the
    Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) over the previous 30 calendar days.
    LTV is calculated on the net weight after stone deduction at assessed purity.

2.4 Maximum LTV Ratio: 75% of assessed gold value (as per RBI Master Circular).

SECTION 3: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

3.1 Identity Proof (any one):
    - Aadhaar Card (mandatory for Indian nationals)
    - PAN Card
    - Passport
    - Voter ID

3.2 Address Proof (any one):
    - Aadhaar Card
    - Utility bill (not older than 3 months)
    - Bank statement (last 3 months)
    - Driving licence

3.3 Income Proof: Not required for gold loans up to INR 5,00,000.
    For loans above INR 5,00,000: Latest ITR or 3 months bank statement.

3.4 Gold Ownership: Self-declaration accepted.
    Note: MNB does not verify third-party gold ownership claims.

SECTION 4: LOAN APPLICATION PROCESS
Step 1: Customer visits branch with gold and KYC documents.
Step 2: Certified valuer assesses gold purity and weight.
Step 3: Loan amount calculated at applicable LTV.
Step 4: Customer selects repayment scheme (Bullet / EMI / OD).
Step 5: Loan agreement executed and gold secured in bank vault.
Step 6: Disbursement within 30 minutes of agreement execution.

SECTION 5: GOLD STORAGE AND SECURITY
Gold is stored in bank-managed vaults under dual-key security.
Insurance cover: Full replacement value covered under MNB's blanket policy.
Customer may inspect pledged gold at any time during branch hours with 48-hour notice.
