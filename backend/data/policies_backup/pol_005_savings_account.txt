SECTION 1: SAVINGS ACCOUNT TYPES AND MINIMUM BALANCE

Account Type                    Monthly Average Balance   Charges if Below MAB
------------------------------  ----------------------    -------------------------
MNB Basic Savings (Zero-Bal)    No minimum required       Nil
MNB Regular Savings             INR 5,000                 INR 150 per month
MNB Premium Savings             INR 25,000                INR 400 per month
MNB Elite Savings               INR 1,00,000              INR 750 per month

Note: Monthly Average Balance (MAB) is calculated as sum of daily closing balances
divided by number of days in the month.

SECTION 2: INTEREST ON SAVINGS ACCOUNTS

Account Balance             Interest Rate (% p.a.)
--------------------------  ----------------------
Up to INR 1,00,000          3.50%
INR 1,00,001 – INR 5,00,000 4.00%
Above INR 5,00,000          4.25%

Interest credited: Quarterly (March, June, September, December)
Calculation: Simple interest on daily closing balance

SECTION 3: DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ACCOUNT OPENING

3.1 For Indian Residents:
    - PAN Card (mandatory)
    - Aadhaar Card (mandatory for KYC)
    - Passport-size photograph (2 copies)
    - Initial deposit cheque or cash

3.2 For NRI Savings (NRE / NRO):
    - Passport (valid)
    - Visa / Residence permit
    - Overseas address proof
    - PAN Card or Form 60

SECTION 4: ACCOUNT FEATURES — MNB PREMIUM SAVINGS
- Free unlimited ATM withdrawals (MNB ATMs and other bank ATMs)
- Free RTGS / NEFT transfers (online banking)
- Demat account linkage at zero cost
- Complimentary international debit card
- 25% discount on locker rental charges
- Dedicated relationship manager for balances above INR 10,00,000

SECTION 5: ACCOUNT CLOSURE CHARGES
- Within 6 months of opening: INR 500
- After 6 months: Nil
- Dormant account reactivation: INR 100 (with valid KYC update)

SECTION 6: INOPERATIVE / DORMANT ACCOUNTS
Account becomes inoperative after 12 months of no customer-initiated transactions.
Account marked dormant after 24 months of inactivity.
Funds transferred to RBI's DEAF fund after 10 years of unclaimed status.
