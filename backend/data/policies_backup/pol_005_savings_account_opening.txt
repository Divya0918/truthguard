1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy governs the onboarding, eligibility criteria, minimum maintenance 
balances, pricing tiers, interest computation formulas, and daily transaction 
ceilings for retail Savings Bank (SB) accounts offered across Meridian National 
Bank's branch and digital banking networks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ACCOUNT OPENING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Savings accounts may be opened by:
1. Individual Resident Indians (single or joint accounts up to 4 holders).
2. Minors aged 10 years and above under self-operation (Meridian Junior Account).
3. Minors below 10 years operated through natural or court-appointed guardians.
4. Non-Resident Indians (under NRE / NRO variants governed by FEMA).
5. Registered Charitable Trusts, Clubs, and Societies (governed by special bye-laws).

Commercial entities, partnerships, and proprietary concerns are legally barred 
from opening savings accounts and must open current accounts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SAVINGS ACCOUNT VARIANTS AND MINIMUM BALANCE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meridian National Bank offers four specialized account variants tailored to 
distinct customer demographics:

+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Account Variant            | Average Monthly (AMB) | Non-Maintenance Fee    |
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Basic Savings (BSBDA)      | INR 0 (Zero-Balance)  | Nil (No penalty)       |
| Classic Retail Savings     | INR 5,000 (Metro/Urb) | INR 250 + GST / month  |
|                            | INR 2,500 (Semi-Urb)  | INR 150 + GST / month  |
|                            | INR 1,000 (Rural)     | INR 75 + GST / month   |
| Meridian Privilege Premium | INR 25,000            | INR 500 + GST / month  |
| Meridian Infinite Elite    | INR 100,000           | INR 1,000 + GST / month|
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+

Calculation of AMB: Sum of daily end-of-day balances divided by total calendar 
days in the billing month.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAVINGS INTEREST RATE SCHEDULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Interest on domestic savings accounts is calculated on a daily closing balance 
basis and credited to accounts on the final calendar day of each quarter 
(March, June, September, December):

+-------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Daily Closing Balance Slab          | Annual Interest Rate (% p.a.)         |
+-------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Up to INR 100,000                   | 3.00% per annum                       |
| Above INR 100,000 up to INR 500,000 | 3.50% per annum                       |
| Above INR 500,000 up to INR 5,000k  | 4.00% per annum                       |
| Above INR 5,000,000 (High-Net)      | 4.50% per annum                       |
+-------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRANSACTION AND ATM WITHDRAWAL LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Daily debit card spending and cash disbursement thresholds:

+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+
| Account Variant        | Daily ATM Cash Limit | Daily POS / E-Commerce Limit|
+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+
| Basic Savings (BSBDA)  | INR 10,000           | INR 10,000                  |
| Classic Retail Savings | INR 40,000           | INR 100,000                 |
| Meridian Privilege     | INR 100,000          | INR 250,000                 |
| Meridian Infinite Elite| INR 250,000          | INR 500,000                 |
+------------------------+----------------------+-----------------------------+

Free ATM Transactions: 5 free transactions per month at MNB ATMs, and 3 free 
transactions at other bank ATMs in metro cities (5 in non-metro locations). 
Subsequent financial transactions are billed at INR 21.00 + GST.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for Identity and Address:
   - Aadhaar number (with physical biometric verification or OTP e-KYC).
   - Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form 60 declaration.
   - Passport, Voter ID Card, or Driving Licence.
2. Two passport-sized photographs.
3. Specimen signature recorded on biometric pad.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Corporate Salary Package: AMB is waived to INR 0 provided salary credit of at 
  least INR 20,000 occurs every consecutive 60 days. If salary ceases for 3 
  consecutive months, the account automatically converts to Classic Retail SB.
