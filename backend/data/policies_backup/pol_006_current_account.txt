SECTION 1: CURRENT ACCOUNT TYPES

Account Type            Quarterly Average Balance   Non-Maintenance Charges
---------------------   -------------------------   -----------------------
MNB Business Basic      INR 10,000 (QAB)            INR 500 per quarter
MNB Business Pro        INR 50,000 (QAB)            INR 1,200 per quarter
MNB Business Premium    INR 2,00,000 (QAB)          INR 2,500 per quarter
MNB Corporate           INR 5,00,000 (QAB)          INR 5,000 per quarter

QAB = Quarterly Average Balance (sum of daily EOD balances / number of days in quarter)

SECTION 2: TRANSACTION LIMITS AND FEES

2.1 Cash Deposit at Branch:
    MNB Business Basic: Free up to INR 2,00,000 per month; INR 2 per INR 1,000 beyond
    MNB Business Pro: Free up to INR 5,00,000 per month; INR 2 per INR 1,000 beyond
    MNB Business Premium: Free up to INR 20,00,000 per month; INR 1.50 per INR 1,000
    MNB Corporate: As per negotiated terms

2.2 NEFT / RTGS:
    Online: Free (unlimited) for all account types
    Branch: INR 5 per NEFT; INR 25 per RTGS transaction

2.3 Cheque Books:
    First cheque book (25 leaves): Free for all account types
    Subsequent: INR 3 per leaf

SECTION 3: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR CURRENT ACCOUNT OPENING

3.1 Sole Proprietor:
    - PAN Card of proprietor and business
    - GST Registration / Shop & Establishment Certificate
    - Aadhaar of proprietor
    - Business address proof

3.2 Private Limited Company:
    - Certificate of Incorporation
    - MOA / AOA
    - Board Resolution authorizing account opening
    - PAN Card of company
    - Aadhaar of all authorized signatories
    - List of directors

3.3 Partnership Firm:
    - Partnership deed
    - PAN Card of firm and partners
    - GST Registration
    - Aadhaar of all partners

SECTION 4: OVERDRAFT FACILITY
Overdraft limit: Up to 2× monthly credit turnover for Business Pro and above.
Interest rate: 14.50% p.a. on utilized OD amount.
Processing fee: 0.50% of OD limit (min INR 2,000).
