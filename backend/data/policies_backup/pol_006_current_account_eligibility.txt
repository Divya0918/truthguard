1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes regulatory norms, entity eligibility criteria, Average 
Quarterly Balance (AQB) schedules, overdraft lending criteria, and non-compliance 
penalties for Current Accounts maintained at Meridian National Bank (MNB).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELIGIBLE BUSINESS ENTITIES & DOCUMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Accounts are designed for non-individual entities and trading individuals:

+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Entity Constitution        | Mandatory Verification Documents               |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Sole Proprietorship        | GST Registration, Shop & Est. License, Udyam   |
| Partnership Firm           | Registered Partnership Deed, Form A/B, GSTIN   |
| Limited Liability Co (LLP) | Certificate of Incorporation, LLP Agreement    |
| Private / Public Limited   | Certificate of Incorp, MOA/AOA, Board Res.,    |
|                            | Beneficial Ownership declaration (>10% share)  |
| Trusts, Societies & Clubs  | Trust Deed, 12A/80G, Managing Committee Res.   |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

Statutory GST Mandate: Valid Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) 
is mandatory for all entities with annual sales/turnover exceeding INR 2,000,000 
(or INR 1,000,000 in special category northeastern states).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MINIMUM AVERAGE QUARTERLY BALANCE (AQB) SCHEDULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current accounts earn 0% interest and require maintenance of the following 
minimum balances computed on a quarterly basis:

+-------------------------+----------------------+----------------------------+
| Current Account Variant | Minimum AQB          | Quarterly Shortfall Fee    |
+-------------------------+----------------------+----------------------------+
| MNB Classic Business    | INR 25,000           | INR 1,500 + GST            |
| MNB Advantage Business  | INR 100,000          | INR 3,000 + GST            |
| MNB Dynamic Corporate   | INR 500,000          | INR 6,000 + GST            |
| MNB Institutional Elite | INR 1,000,000        | INR 10,000 + GST           |
+-------------------------+----------------------+----------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SECURED & UNSECURED OVERDRAFT (OD) CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eligible commercial account holders may apply for Clean or Secured Overdraft limits:
- Vintage Requirement: Business track record of at least 24 consecutive operating months.
- Clean (Unsecured) OD: Up to 20% of audited annual banking turnover, max INR 2,500,000. 
  Interest rate: Base Rate + 4.50% (effective 13.50% p.a.).
- Secured OD: Against fixed deposits or commercial property (up to 70% collateral 
  value). Interest rate: Deposit rate + 1.50% or Base Rate + 1.75%.
- Annual Review: All OD facilities are subject to mandatory annual renewal with 
  submission of fresh audited financials and tax returns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHEQUE RETURN & DISHONOR CHARGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cheque dishonor represents a serious breach of credit discipline:
- Inward Cheque Dishonor (Financial / Insufficient Funds):
  - 1st Return in Quarter: INR 500 per instrument.
  - 2nd Return in Quarter: INR 750 per instrument.
  - 3rd and subsequent returns: INR 1,000 per instrument.
- Outward Cheque Dishonor (Technical / Signature Mismatch): INR 200 flat.
- Repeated Dishonor Rule: If 4 financial cheque bounces occur within a calendar 
  quarter, the cheque book facility is suspended, and the account is flagged for review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- DPIIT Registered Startups: Zero-AQB exemption for the first 12 months from 
  incorporation date upon producing DPIIT recognition certificate.
- Escrow / RERA Designated Accounts: Excluded from general AQB shortfall levies.
