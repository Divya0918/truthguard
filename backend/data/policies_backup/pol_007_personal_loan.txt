SECTION 1: PERSONAL LOAN PRODUCT OVERVIEW
MNB offers unsecured personal loans for personal needs including medical expenses,
travel, home renovation, education, and debt consolidation.

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Category            Minimum Salary    Age Range    Credit Score   Max Loan
-----------------   --------------    ----------   ------------   ----------
Salaried (Pvt)      INR 25,000/mo     21–58 yrs    700+           INR 20 lakh
Salaried (Govt)     INR 20,000/mo     21–60 yrs    680+           INR 25 lakh
Self-employed       INR 3,00,000/yr   25–65 yrs    720+           INR 15 lakh
MNB Salary Acct     INR 20,000/mo     21–58 yrs    No minimum     INR 30 lakh

SECTION 3: INTEREST RATE STRUCTURE

Credit Score        Rate Band (% p.a.)   Processing Fee
-----------------   ------------------   --------------
800 and above       10.50% – 11.00%      1.00% (max INR 5,000)
750 – 799           11.00% – 12.00%      1.50%
700 – 749           12.00% – 13.50%      2.00%
Below 700           Not eligible
MNB Staff           8.50% flat           Nil

Rates are on reducing balance method.

SECTION 4: LOAN PARAMETERS
Minimum loan: INR 50,000
Maximum loan: INR 30,00,000 (subject to eligibility)
Minimum tenure: 12 months
Maximum tenure: 60 months (5 years)
Prepayment: Allowed after 12 EMIs; 3% charge on outstanding principal.

SECTION 5: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- PAN Card, Aadhaar Card
- Last 3 months salary slips (salaried) or 2 years ITR (self-employed)
- 6 months bank statement
- Employer ID or appointment letter (salaried)
