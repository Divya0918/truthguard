--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes underwriting parameters, credit bureau benchmarks, 
borrowing capacity matrices, and interest rate tiers for unsecured personal 
loans granted to individual borrowers by Meridian National Bank (MNB).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BORROWER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Applicants must fulfill each of the baseline criteria outlined below:

+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter                  | Salaried Applicants     | Self-Employed / Prof |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Age at Application         | Min 21 years            | Min 25 years         |
| Age at Loan Maturity       | Max 60 years (or retire)| Max 65 years         |
| Minimum Net Monthly Income | INR 30,000 (Metro/Tier1)| INR 50,000 (Profit)  |
|                            | INR 25,000 (Other cities| Min INR 600k ITR p.a.|
| Employment Stability       | Min 1 year continuous   | Min 3 years in business|
| Minimum CIBIL TransUnion   | Score >= 700            | Score >= 720         |
| Fixed Obligation to Income | Max 50% FOIR (Debt/Inc) | Max 45% FOIR         |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

Credit Bureau Threshold: Any applicant with a CIBIL score strictly below 700 is 
system-rejected. Hard inquiries exceeding 4 in the preceding 90 days trigger an 
automatic manual risk review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LOAN QUANTUM, TENURE & REPAYMENT OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Personal loans are sanctioned as clean credit without physical collateral:
- Minimum Loan Amount: INR 50,000.
- Maximum Loan Amount: INR 2,500,000 (up to INR 4,000,000 for elite corporate employees).
- Tenure Range: Minimum 12 months up to maximum 60 months (5 years) in 6-month steps.
- Repayment: Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) collected via National Automated 
  Clearing House (NACH) e-mandate or internal standing instruction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RISK-BASED INTEREST RATE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Interest rates are pegged to the applicant's CIBIL score and corporate employer category:

+----------------------+--------------------+---------------------------------+
| CIBIL Score Tier     | Corporate Tier A/B | Other Salaried & Self-Employed  |
+----------------------+--------------------+---------------------------------+
| 775 and above        | 10.50% - 11.25%    | 11.75% - 12.50%                 |
| 730 to 774           | 11.50% - 12.50%    | 12.75% - 13.75%                 |
| 700 to 729           | 13.00% - 14.25%    | 14.50% - 15.50%                 |
| Below 700            | Ineligible / Decl  | Ineligible / Declined           |
+----------------------+--------------------+---------------------------------+

Note: Rates are fixed for the entire loan tenor and computed on a reducing balance basis.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROCESSING FEES & FORECLOSURE CHARGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Processing Fee: 1.50% to 2.50% of the gross sanctioned loan amount (minimum 
  INR 2,500, maximum capped at INR 15,000) plus applicable GST.
- Pre-payment / Foreclosure Fees:
  - Within 12 months of disbursement: 4.00% of outstanding principal + GST.
  - Between 13 to 24 months: 3.00% of outstanding principal + GST.
  - After 24 months: 2.00% of outstanding principal + GST.
  - Part-prepayment: Up to 25% of principal allowed once per financial year after 12 EMIs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Existing MNB Home Loan Borrowers: Concessional interest rate discount of 0.50% 
  with pre-approved top-up eligibility if home loan vintage exceeds 24 clean payments.
- Defense Personnel: Flat interest rate of 10.25% p.a. with 100% waiver of processing fee.
