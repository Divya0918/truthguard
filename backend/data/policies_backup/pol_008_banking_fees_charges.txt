1. GENERAL POLICY APPLICABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This schedule establishes the standardized fee structure and service charges levied 
by Meridian National Bank (MNB) across all consumer and commercial banking products. 
All charges are subject to statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) at prevailing rates 
(currently 18%) unless explicitly specified as tax-exempt.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACCOUNT MAINTENANCE & ANNUAL CHARGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Classic Debit Card Annual Maintenance Fee: INR 200.00 + GST.
- Platinum / International Debit Card Annual Fee: INR 500.00 + GST.
- Inactive / Dormant Account Conversion Fee: Strictly Nil (prohibited by regulator).
- Account Closure Charges: Free if closed within 14 days of opening; INR 500 if 
  closed between 15 days and 12 months; free after 12 months of operation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA): Zero charges on all electronic 
  transfers, zero debit card annual maintenance fees, and free quarterly statements.
- Senior Citizens (Aged 60+): 50% concession on physical statement charges and 
  demand draft issuance fees.
