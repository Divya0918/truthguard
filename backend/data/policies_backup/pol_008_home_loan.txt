SECTION 1: HOME LOAN PRODUCTS

1.1 MNB GrihaShakti — Standard Home Purchase Loan
1.2 MNB GrihaShakti Plus — Balance Transfer + Top-Up
1.3 MNB BuildIt — Self-Construction Loan
1.4 MNB RenovateNow — Home Renovation Loan

SECTION 2: INTEREST RATES (Floating, Linked to MNB Base Rate 8.50%)

Loan Category                           Rate (% p.a., Floating)
--------------------------------------  -----------------------
Loan up to INR 30 lakh                  8.75%
Loan INR 30 lakh – INR 75 lakh         9.00%
Loan INR 75 lakh – INR 1.5 crore       9.25%
Loan above INR 1.5 crore               9.50%
Women applicants (primary borrower)     -0.05% concession on above rates

Fixed rate option: 10.50% p.a. for first 3 years; floating thereafter.

SECTION 3: LTV RATIOS FOR HOME LOANS

Property Cost                LTV (Max)
--------------------------   ---------
Up to INR 30 lakh            90%
INR 30 lakh – INR 75 lakh   80%
Above INR 75 lakh            75%

SECTION 4: ELIGIBILITY
Minimum age: 21 years; Maximum age at loan maturity: 70 years (salaried), 75 years (self-employed with co-applicant).
Minimum income: INR 3,00,000 per annum (individual or joint).
CIBIL score: Minimum 700.
Maximum tenure: 30 years (360 months).

SECTION 5: CHARGES
Processing fee: 0.50% of loan amount (min INR 2,500; max INR 15,000).
Prepayment (floating rate): Nil (as per RBI guidelines).
Prepayment (fixed rate): 2% of outstanding principal within fixed-rate period.
