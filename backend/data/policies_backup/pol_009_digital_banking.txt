SECTION 1: MOBILE BANKING — MNB SmartApp
Daily transaction limit (IMPS/UPI): INR 2,00,000 per day
Fund transfer daily limit (NEFT): INR 10,00,000 per day
Bill payment monthly limit: INR 5,00,000
Self-account transfer: Unlimited

SECTION 2: INTERNET BANKING — MNB NetBanking

2.1 Transaction Limits
NEFT: INR 10,00,000 per day per beneficiary; INR 25,00,000 per day total
RTGS: Minimum INR 2,00,000; Maximum INR 2,00,00,000 per transaction
UPI (linked): INR 1,00,000 per transaction; INR 1,00,000 per day

2.2 New Beneficiary Cooling Period
First transfer to a new beneficiary: Maximum INR 25,000 in first 24 hours.
Cooling period waived for existing MNB account holders added as beneficiaries.

SECTION 3: UPI — MNB @upi
UPI ID format: registered_mobile@mnb
Daily limit: INR 1,00,000 (per RBI guidelines)
Per transaction: INR 1,00,000
Merchant payments via UPI: Up to INR 2,00,000 (special category merchants)
UPI Lite (offline): INR 500 per transaction; INR 4,000 wallet balance

SECTION 4: SECURITY POLICY
4.1 MNB will NEVER ask for OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords via call, email, or SMS.
4.2 Report suspicious calls immediately: Toll-free 1800-123-4567
4.3 Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all transactions above INR 5,000.
4.4 Device binding: Each account may be linked to a maximum of 2 mobile devices.
4.5 Auto-block: Account blocked after 5 consecutive wrong MPIN attempts.

SECTION 5: CHARGES FOR DIGITAL TRANSACTIONS
IMPS (via app): Free up to 10 transactions per month; INR 5 per transaction thereafter
NEFT (online): Free
RTGS (online): Free
UPI: Free (as per NPCI guidelines)
SMS alerts: INR 15 per month (waived for Premium and Elite savings accounts)
