1. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy outlines the Know Your Customer (KYC), Customer Due Diligence (CDD), 
and customer identification procedures enforced by Meridian National Bank (MNB) 
in strict adherence to the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) and central 
banking Master Directions. MNB maintains zero tolerance for anonymous accounts, 
benami holdings, and illicit capital flows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. OFFICIALLY VALID DOCUMENTS (OVD) MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Individual customers must establish both Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of 
Address (POA) using the approved documents specified below:

+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Document Name              | Recognized Utility / Classification            |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Aadhaar Card / e-Aadhaar   | Valid for POI and POA (with first 8 digits     |
|                            | masked on physical copies per privacy rules)   |
| Permanent Account Number   | Valid for POI only. Mandatory for all cash     |
| (PAN) Card / e-PAN         | transactions > INR 50,000 & high-value deposits|
| Passport (Machine Readable)| Valid for POI and POA (Must be currently valid)|
| Voter Identity Card (EPIC) | Valid for POI and POA (Issued by Election Comm)|
| Driving Licence (Smartcard)| Valid for POI and POA (Must not be expired)    |
| NREGA Job Card             | Valid for POI and POA (Duly signed by Officer) |
+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

Deemed Proofs of Address (for temporary address, valid for max 90 days until OVD 
address update): Utility bills (electricity/piped gas/water bill not older than 
60 days), property tax receipt, or municipal tax voucher.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VIDEO-BASED CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION PROCESS (V-CIP / VIDEO KYC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MNB offers end-to-end digital onboarding via Video KYC under rigorous security 
parameters:
1. Geolocation Verification: Real-time Geo-tagging confirms applicant is physically 
   situated within the territorial borders of India during call execution.
2. Live Interaction: Live bi-directional audio-visual interaction between certified 
   MNB bank official and applicant.
3. Facial Matching & Liveness: AI-driven facial recognition algorithms compare 
   applicant's live face against the photograph on Aadhaar/PAN with minimum 95% 
   confidence score. Liveness detection detects spoofing and recorded videos.
4. Document Capture: Clear high-resolution live capture of physical PAN card and 
   wet-ink signature on plain white paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PERIODIC KYC UPDATION (RE-KYC) SCHEDULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every customer account is classified into a risk tier upon opening, dictating the 
statutory frequency of mandatory KYC refresh:

+------------------+-----------------------+----------------------------------+
| Risk Tier        | Customer Profile      | Mandatory Re-KYC Frequency       |
+------------------+-----------------------+----------------------------------+
| High Risk        | PEPs, Non-Residents,  | Once every 2 Years               |
|                  | High-net bullion/cash |                                  |
| Medium Risk      | Salaried/SME traders  | Once every 8 Years               |
| Low Risk         | Standard retail CASA, | Once every 10 Years              |
|                  | Pensioners, Students  |                                  |
+------------------+-----------------------+----------------------------------+

Low-risk individuals with no change of address may complete Re-KYC via self-declaration 
submitted through internet banking, mobile app, or ATM touchpoints.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE & ACCOUNT FREEZING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where a customer fails to furnish updated KYC documents after receipt of 3 sequential 
notices (served at 30-day intervals):
1. Day 90: Partial Freezing imposed (Credits permitted, debits completely blocked).
2. Day 180: Total Freezing imposed (Both debits and credits completely blocked).
3. Day 360: Account closure proceedings initiated, funds segregated to suspense ledger.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Small Accounts: Individuals lacking OVD may open a "Small Account" with self-attested 
  photograph, subject to aggregate credits <= INR 100,000/year, maximum balance 
  <= INR 50,000, and 12-month window to procure standard OVD.
