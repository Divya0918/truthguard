SECTION 1: KYC FREQUENCY AND PERIODIC UPDATE

Customer Risk Category    KYC Update Frequency
----------------------    --------------------
Low Risk                  Every 10 years
Medium Risk               Every 8 years
High Risk                 Every 2 years

Risk categorization is based on transaction patterns, geography, occupation, and
account vintage as per RBI KYC Master Directions, 2016 (updated 2023).

SECTION 2: OFFICIALLY VALID DOCUMENTS (OVDs) — INDIA

Category        Accepted Documents
-----------     --------------------------------------------------
Identity        Aadhaar (UIDAI-verified), PAN Card, Passport,
                Voter ID, Driving Licence, NREGA Job Card
Address         Aadhaar, Utility Bills (≤3 months), Bank Statement,
                Rental Agreement (registered), Employer letter

SECTION 3: VIDEO KYC (V-KYC) PROCESS
MNB offers V-KYC for account opening without branch visit.
Requirements:
- Stable internet connection
- Original PAN Card and Aadhaar
- Live facial recognition match with Aadhaar photo
- Digital signature via OTP

V-KYC supported for: Savings accounts, FDs, Credit cards, Personal loans.

SECTION 4: AML — SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTION REPORTING

4.1 Cash transactions of INR 10,00,000 or above in a single day must be reported
    to the Financial Intelligence Unit — India (FIU-IND) under PMLA, 2002.

4.2 Structuring (splitting transactions to avoid reporting threshold) is prohibited
    and monitored via automated AML systems.

4.3 Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) must be filed within 7 working days of
    the suspicious transaction being identified.

4.4 Customer must not be tipped off about any STR filing.

SECTION 5: BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP (BO) FOR BUSINESSES
For accounts with a legal entity, beneficial owners (those with ≥25% shareholding
or controlling interest) must complete individual KYC.
Shell companies from FATF blacklisted / greylisted jurisdictions: Account opening
is prohibited as per MNB policy.
