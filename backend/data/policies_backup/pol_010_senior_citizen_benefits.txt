1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meridian National Bank (MNB) is committed to providing preferential, dignified, 
and accessible financial services to elder citizens. This policy consolidates the 
special interest benefits, priority service channels, door-step amenities, and 
compliance relaxations reserved for senior customers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELIGIBILITY THRESHOLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Any individual resident customer who has attained 60 completed years of age 
(or 55 years in the case of individuals who have taken voluntary retirement / superannuation 
under approved defense or public service schemes) is classified as a Senior Citizen. 
Super-Senior Citizen status is conferred upon reaching 80 completed years of age.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FINANCIAL BENEFITS & INTEREST RATE CONCESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Senior citizens enjoy enhanced yields and reduced fee schedules across core services:

+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Service / Product          | Standard Customer     | Senior Citizen Benefit |
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
| Fixed Deposits (All Tenors)| Standard Card Rate    | Card Rate + 0.50% p.a. |
| Super Senior FD (>5 Yrs)   | Standard Card Rate    | Card Rate + 0.75% p.a. |
| Savings Account AMB Waiver | Standard AMB applies  | 50% reduced AMB rule   |
| Premature FD Liquidation   | 1.00% or 0.50% penalty| 0.25% penalty waiver   |
| Demand Drafts (DD)         | Standard Fee          | 50% discount on charges|
| Physical Account Statement | INR 100 per request   | Free quarterly branch  |
| Locker Rental Discount     | Standard Rental       | 10% annual concession  |
+----------------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DOORSTEP BANKING SERVICES (DSB)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Senior citizens aged 70 years and above, as well as differently-abled elder citizens, 
can access Doorstep Banking Services up to 3 times per calendar month free of fee:
- Cash pickup and delivery up to INR 25,000 per day.
- Pick up of cheques, demand drafts, and life certificates (Jeevan Pramaan).
- Delivery of demand drafts, account statements, and cheque books.
- Submission of Form 15G / 15H for tax deduction exemption.

Bookings can be initiated through the MNB Mobile App, dedicated toll-free helpline, 
or local branch appointment desk.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DEDICATED ASSISTANCE & PRIORITY BRANCH BANKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dedicated Toll-Free Priority Helpline: 1800-555-MNB-SR (Available 24x7 with 
   zero IVR queue wait times, routing directly to senior human agents).
2. Priority Service Counters: All tier-1 and tier-2 MNB branches must maintain a 
   dedicated "Elder Care Desk" equipped with comfortable ergonomic seating.
3. Relaxed Re-KYC: In-person branch visits are not mandatory. Senior citizens may 
   complete periodic KYC via video link or authorized agent doorstep visits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PENSION ACCOUNT PRIVILEGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Holders of central/state government pension accounts with MNB enjoy zero minimum 
balance requirements, unlimited free ATM withdrawals across all domestic bank ATMs, 
and automated annual submission of Digital Life Certificates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. EXCEPTIONS & RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Preferential FD interest rate (+0.50%) is not applicable to non-individual accounts, 
  corporate accounts, or NRE / FCNR foreign currency deposit accounts.
