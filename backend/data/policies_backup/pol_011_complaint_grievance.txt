1. PURPOSE & FAIR BANKING COMMITMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meridian National Bank (MNB) is committed to transparent, fair, and prompt resolution 
of customer grievances. This document defines official channels of complaint receipt, 
turnaround time (TAT) service level agreements, escalation stages, and financial 
compensation rules for service deficiencies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MULTI-CHANNEL COMPLAINT SUBMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Customers can register grievances through any of the following touchpoints:
1. Branch Channel: Written complaint recorded in the physical Branch Complaint Register 
   or handed to the Branch Service Manager. A unique acknowledgement ticket is handed over.
2. Voice / Contact Center: 24x7 Customer Care toll-free line at 1800-419-MNB (1800-419-662).
3. Electronic Mail: Dedicated email desk at `grievance.redressal@meridian-nb.com`.
4. Digital Portal / Mobile Banking: In-app support ticketing via "Raise a Dispute" feature.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RESOLUTION TIMELINES (SERVICE LEVEL BENCHMARKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Complaints are classified based on operational complexity and investigated under 
strict statutory timelines:

+-----------------------------+--------------------+--------------------------+
| Category of Complaint       | Typical Issues     | Maximum Resolution TAT   |
+-----------------------------+--------------------+--------------------------+
| Level 1: Simple / Ops       | ATM cash non-disp, | T + 3 Working Days       |
|                             | SMS alert failure  | (Target: 72 hours)       |
| Level 2: Intermediary       | POS / UPI decline, | T + 7 Working Days       |
|                             | erroneous charges  |                          |
| Level 3: Complex / Forensic | Fraudulent trans., | T + 15 Working Days      |
|                             | unauthorized debit |                          |
| Level 4: Credit Bureau      | CIBIL reporting    | T + 30 Calendar Days     |
|                             | correction dispute | (Statutory limit)        |
+-----------------------------+--------------------+--------------------------+

Note: "T" represents the date of formal logging and ticket generation in the MNB 
central grievance portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FOUR-TIER ESCALATION MATRIX & BANKING OMBUDSMAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a complaint remains unresolved or the complainant is dissatisfied with the response:

- Tier 1: Branch Manager / Frontline Support Desk (Resolution within T+3 days).
- Tier 2: Regional Grievance Redressal Officer (RGRO) (Resolution within T+7 days).
- Tier 3: Principal Nodal Officer (PNO) & Internal Ombudsman (IO) located at 
  Corporate Headquarters, Mumbai.
- Tier 4: Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RBI-OS): If the bank fails to 
  resolve the complaint within 30 calendar days from initial lodging date, or if the 
  customer receives an unsatisfactory rejection, the customer may approach the 
  Reserve Bank Banking Ombudsman online at https://cms.rbi.org.in.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CUSTOMER COMPENSATION POLICY FOR FAILED TRANSACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In compliance with central banking Harmonization of TAT guidelines:
- ATM Cash Dispense Failures: If customer account is debited but cash is not dispensed, 
  and the bank fails to reverse within T+5 calendar days, MNB pays compensation of 
  INR 100 per day of delay directly into the customer's account.
- Failed UPI / IMPS Transfers: Auto-reversal within T+1 day. Delay beyond T+1 
  incurs INR 100 per day penalty.
- Unauthorized Electronic Fraud: Zero liability for customer if reported within 
  3 working days of occurrence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Complaints pending before courts, consumer forums, or statutory judicial tribunals 
  fall outside the administrative grievance redressal jurisdiction.
