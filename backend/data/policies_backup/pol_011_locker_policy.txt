SECTION 1: LOCKER SIZES AND ANNUAL RENT

Locker Size    Dimensions (HxWxD cm)    Annual Rent (INR)
-----------    ---------------------    -----------------
Small          10 × 23 × 45             INR 1,500
Medium         20 × 23 × 45             INR 2,500
Large          30 × 45 × 45             INR 4,500
Extra Large    60 × 45 × 45             INR 8,000

Premium savings and Elite savings account holders receive 25% discount on locker rent.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT (REFUNDABLE)
Small: INR 3,000 | Medium: INR 5,000 | Large: INR 9,000 | Extra Large: INR 15,000
Security deposit is refundable on closure of locker without interest.

SECTION 3: ACCESS CONDITIONS
3.1 Operating hours: Branch banking hours (typically 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM, Mon–Sat)
3.2 Saturday access: Available at designated branches only
3.3 Joint locker: Requires both holders to be present unless "Either or Survivor" mandate
3.4 Maximum access frequency: Unlimited (subject to branch capacity)

SECTION 4: NOMINATION
Nomination is mandatory at the time of locker allotment.
Nominees may access locker only on death of all holders with death certificate and
valid identity proof. Valuables handed over under panchanama.

SECTION 5: LOCKER BREAK-OPEN POLICY
If rent is unpaid for 3 consecutive years:
1. MNB sends 3 registered notices (30 days apart)
2. If no response, locker is broken open in presence of two independent witnesses
3. Contents inventoried, sealed, and stored for 5 years
4. Unclaimed contents are auctioned; proceeds deposited with RBI

SECTION 6: LIABILITY LIMITATION
MNB is not responsible for loss of contents due to natural disasters, war, or acts
of God. Liability for bank negligence is limited to 100 times the annual locker rent.
Customers are advised to insure locker contents independently.
