SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document establishes the revised interest rate schedule for Fixed Deposit (FD)
accounts at Meridian National Bank, effective January 1, 2024. This version (v2.0)
supersedes the previous schedule (POL-001, v1.0, dated 2023-01-01). All new FDs
opened on or after January 1, 2024 attract rates as specified in this document.
FDs opened before this date will continue at the rates prevailing at time of booking
until maturity, after which renewal rates will be as per this schedule.

SECTION 2: REVISED STANDARD INTEREST RATE SCHEDULE (2024 — CURRENT)

-----------         ---------------------   ----------------------------

15 – 30 days        4.25%                   4.75%

KEY CHANGE FROM v1.0: All rates increased by 0.25% – 0.50% p.a. to reflect
current monetary policy environment.

SECTION 3: SENIOR CITIZEN BENEFIT (REVISED)
Senior citizens (age 60 years and above) receive an additional 0.50% per annum
above the general rate. This benefit is unchanged from v1.0.
Proof of age (Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport) is required at account opening.
Senior citizen rates are marked in the schedule above.

SECTION 4: SPECIAL FD CATEGORIES (2024)

4.1 Tax Saver FD "MNB Shield-5"
    Tenure: Exactly 5 years (mandatory lock-in, no premature withdrawal)
    Rate: 7.50% p.a. (general) | 8.00% p.a. (senior citizen)
    Maximum investment: INR 1,50,000 per financial year per PAN
    Tax benefit: Deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961
    Interest payout: Quarterly (cannot be changed post-booking)

4.2 Flexi FD "MNB SmartDeposit"
    Linked to primary savings account with sweep-in / sweep-out facility
    Minimum FD amount: INR 25,000
    Rate: Prevailing tenure rate at time of sweep-in
    Auto-renewal: At rate prevailing on maturity date

4.3 Recurring Deposit (RD) — For Reference
    Minimum monthly installment: INR 500
    Rates: Same as FD rates for corresponding accumulated tenure
    Premature closure rules same as FD

SECTION 5: PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL PENALTY MATRIX (2024)

Tenure Held         Penalty Deduction from Applicable FD Rate
----------------    ------------------------------------------
Less than 3 months  Nil interest; principal returned in full
3 – 6 months        1.00% deducted from the applicable rate
6 – 12 months       0.75% deducted from the applicable rate
More than 12 months 0.50% deducted from the applicable rate

Example: FD booked at 6.75% p.a. for 2 years, withdrawn after 18 months.
Applicable rate for 18 months tenure = 6.75%. Penalty = 0.50%. Effective rate = 6.25%.

SECTION 6: INTEREST CALCULATION METHOD
6.1 Tenure up to 12 months: Simple interest.
6.2 Tenure above 12 months: Quarterly compounded interest.
6.3 Interest payout options: Monthly, Quarterly, Half-yearly, At maturity.
6.4 TDS: Deducted at source per current Income Tax rules when annual interest
    exceeds INR 40,000 (INR 50,000 for senior citizens).

SECTION 7: RENEWAL POLICY
7.1 Auto-renewal: At the rate prevailing on the maturity date for the same tenure.
7.2 Renewal notice: MNB will send reminders 30 days and 7 days before maturity.
7.3 Grace period: 14 days from maturity date to claim without loss of interest.

SECTION 8: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
These rates are set in accordance with RBI guidelines and MNB Board approval
dated December 15, 2023. Rates are subject to periodic review.
